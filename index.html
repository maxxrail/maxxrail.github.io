01 — About

I like hard problems with measurable answers — the kind you solve with an oscilloscope, a profiler, or a rewrite.

My range runs from the metal up — writing algorithms to benchmark ultra-low-power RF silicon and soldering ASICs at a semiconductor startup, then owning the architecture of a live event-ticketing app as its CTO. I studied Computer Engineering at McGill with a minor in Applied Artificial Intelligence, and I move comfortably across the stack: systems, web, and the ML layer that increasingly ties them together — computer vision, NLP, and reinforcement learning included.

My default is code that's easy to reason about — clear abstractions, predictable behavior, nothing clever for its own sake. Away from the editor I'm a varsity Track & Field athlete, a snowboarder, and a sailor. Right now I'm looking for software engineering roles.
